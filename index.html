About Us

Woblema Heritage Cultural Troupe LBG is a leading Ghanaian cultural NGO committed to the preservation and global promotion of Ghana's intangible cultural heritage.

The name "Woblema" comes from the Ga language and means "History of our fathers". It reflects our mission: to bring the soul of Ghana to audiences near and far.

Founded in 2017 by a Ghanaian businessman and a musician, Woblema began as a grassroots community ensemble. It has grown into a 35-member professional troupe of master drummers, traditional dancers, vocalists and folk instrumentalists. We are registered as a non-profit, Limited by Guarantee, and operate from Accra, Ghana.

What We Do

We perform, we teach, we preserve.

🥁
We Perform

Choreographed dance, thunderous drums, call-and-response vocals and vibrant traditional regalia. We have graced stages at state functions, international conferences and community durbars.

📚
We Teach

Through our NGO arm we train young people in traditional music, dance, costume design and instrument making.

🌍
We Preserve

We keep Ghana's intangible cultural heritage alive for the next generation through professional artistic excellence.

Festivals & Stages

We are proud regular performers at Ghana's major national festivals, as well as Beach Holiday Cultural Music Shows across all 16 regions of Ghana.

Our Social Impact

Beyond performance, our social impact lies in youth development. Through our NGO arm, we operate:

Weekly training in traditional music and dance for youth aged 12-25.

Bringing cultural education into basic schools.

Training young women in cultural performance and costume design.

Apprenticeship in making traditional drums like the Fontomfrom, Atumpan and Kpanlogo drums.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a world-class cultural institution that sustains Ghanaian heritage while creating jobs and pride for the next generation.

Our Mission

To preserve Ghanaian cultural heritage through professional artistic excellence, youth empowerment, and community engagement.
